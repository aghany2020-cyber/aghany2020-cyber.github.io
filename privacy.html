Privacy Policy — Cookies Studio

Effective 2026-11-22 · Cookies Studio home

1. Who this policy is for

Cookies Studio is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “Cookies Diary” (يوميات كوكيز) on the owner’s own computer. The only Google account that connects to it is the channel owner’s account. This policy explains exactly what the tool does with the data it can access. Questions or complaints: cookies.studio.contact@gmail.com.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

Cookies Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Cookies Studio you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What Google user data we access

With the owner’s consent, Cookies Studio requests only these OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload videos to the owner’s channel;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — read the owner’s channel and video information.

Through these it accesses: the channel’s ID and title; the IDs, titles, tags, privacy/processing status and public statistics (views, likes, comments, subscriber count) of the owner’s own channel and the videos uploaded with the tool; and an OAuth access/refresh token issued by Google. It does not access other users’ data, private messages, comments’ authors, contacts, email, or any other Google service.

4. How the data is used

  • to upload the owner’s videos to the owner’s channel (always with an explicit privacy setting);
  • to check, right after each upload, that the video reached the correct channel with the expected privacy setting;
  • to avoid uploading the same video twice (the tool keeps the YouTube ID of each video it uploaded);
  • to show the owner their own view and subscriber counts on a local dashboard and in local weekly reports.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not combined with other datasets. Cookies Studio itself does not use it to train or improve any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model. Statistics are stored as returned by YouTube; summary figures such as changes between two readings are calculated locally from them and are not presented as figures provided by YouTube.

5. Where the data is stored and how it is protected

  • All data is stored only on the owner’s computer: the OAuth token and the channel’s ID and title in local files, the statistics and upload records in a local database. There is no Cookies Studio server, and nothing is uploaded to any cloud storage by the tool.
  • Communication with Google uses HTTPS (industry-standard transport encryption).
  • The local dashboard listens only on 127.0.0.1 and is not reachable from other devices.
  • The tool sets no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising code. This website itself sets no cookies.

6. Sharing

Cookies Studio does not sell or share Google user data with any third party and has no server of its own. One disclosure for completeness: the owner sometimes asks an AI assistant (Anthropic’s Claude, used under the owner’s own account) to help operate the tool and review its local reports; when the owner does so, the assistant may read those reports, which include the channel’s own view and subscriber counts. This happens only at the owner’s request, for the owner’s own channel management, and that assistant’s handling of conversations is governed by its provider’s terms and the owner’s account settings. The tool’s automatic script-writing feature does not send YouTube API data to the assistant.

7. Retention and deletion

  • The OAuth token is kept only as long as the owner keeps the tool connected, and is used only for the purposes above.
  • Statistics and the YouTube IDs of uploaded videos are kept while the owner’s authorization remains valid. Every successful authorized call re-confirms the authorization; if authorization has not been re-confirmed for 30 days (for example because access was revoked), the tool’s daily routine deletes the stored statistics and YouTube video IDs.
  • The owner can delete everything at any time with the tool’s “disconnect” command, which revokes the token at Google and immediately deletes the local token, the stored channel information, the statistics and the YouTube video IDs. Any deletion request sent to the contact address below is completed within 7 days.

8. Revoking access

You can revoke Cookies Studio’s access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the tool can no longer access YouTube, and its stored YouTube API data is deleted as described above.

9. Third-party content and advertising

Cookies Studio does not allow third parties to serve content or advertisements.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Contact the developer about privacy questions or complaints: cookies.studio.contact@gmail.com.
