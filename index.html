🐾 Cookies Studio

The private production & publishing tool of the YouTube channel “Cookies Diary” — يوميات كوكيز

What Cookies Studio is

Cookies Studio is a desktop application that runs only on the channel owner’s own Windows computer. It is used by one person — the owner of the “Cookies Diary” channel — to prepare short videos about a cat named Cookies and to upload them to the owner’s own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Media library: organizes the owner’s own videos (filmed or generated by the owner with Google Flow) and keeps a record of where each file came from.
  • Rights & quality checks: before anything is uploaded, it checks resolution, length and audio, and verifies that the soundtrack belongs to the owner. Videos that fail are held for the owner’s review.
  • Uploading to YouTube: with the owner’s permission it uploads the owner’s videos to the owner’s channel using the YouTube Data API. During testing every upload is Private; videos made with AI tools are marked with YouTube’s “altered or synthetic content” disclosure.
  • Scheduling & reports: keeps a publishing schedule and shows the channel’s own view and subscriber counts on a local dashboard (http://127.0.0.1, visible only on the owner’s computer).

How it uses Google / YouTube access

Cookies Studio uses YouTube API Services. When the owner connects it, Google asks for two permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the owner’s videos to the owner’s channel;
  • youtube.readonly — to read the owner’s own channel and video statistics and to confirm that an upload arrived with the right privacy setting.

It does not request permission to edit or delete videos, manage the channel, or access any other Google service. Details are in the Privacy Policy. By using Cookies Studio you agree to the Terms of Use, which include the YouTube Terms of Service.
